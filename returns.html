14-Day Returns

Unworn items with tags attached may be returned within 14 days of delivery for a full refund or exchange.

How to Return

  • Email returns@eyeson.com.np with your order number
  • We arrange free pickup inside the valley
  • Refunds are issued within 5 business days of inspection

Non-Returnable

For hygiene reasons, accessories such as caps and innerwear cannot be returned.
